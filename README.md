AI Smart Study Assistant
An AI-powered academic support tool designed to enhance students' learning experiences through intelligent and interactive study features. Built with PHP, HTML5, CSS3, and the NVIDIA AI API.

Overview
The Smart Study Assistant provides students with a comprehensive suite of tools to make studying more efficient, personalized, and interactive. From instant AI-powered academic assistance to automated quiz generation and handwritten notes conversion, the system addresses common student learning challenges in one unified platform.

Features
AI Chatbot
Ask academic questions and receive instant, context-aware responses powered by the NVIDIA AI API. Supports follow-up questions and maintains conversation context for a seamless learning experience.
Quiz & Exam Emulator
Enter a topic or upload study materials (PDF, Word, or slides) and select an assessment format — quiz, mid-semester, or exam. Questions are timed and automatically marked with scores provided upon completion.
Document Analyzer
Upload study materials to have them analyzed by the AI for in-depth discussion or used as the basis for generated quizzes and assessments.
Notes Converter (OCR)
Capture handwritten notes as images and convert them into clean, editable text using OCR technology. Converted notes can be saved as Word or PDF files.
Resource Recommender
Receive curated PDF documents and YouTube video recommendations based on topics the student finds challenging, enabling targeted self-study.
Flashcard Generator
Generate short memory-based flashcards from any selected topic for quick revision and last-minute studying.
